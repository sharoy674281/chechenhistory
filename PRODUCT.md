# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary readers are Chechens in Chechnya and across the diaspora who want an accessible, serious account of their history. Secondary readers include students, families, teachers, researchers, and anyone approaching Chechen history without specialist knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Create a living, multilingual historical book from deep antiquity to the present. The project joins archaeology, linguistics, written archives, material culture, and oral memory while making the strength and limits of every claim visible. Success means a reader can understand the broad chronology, follow a subject in depth, inspect the sources, and return as the archive grows.

## Positioning

A history of Chechnya reconstructed in public: readable as a book, navigable as a timeline, and honest about what survives, what is disputed, and what has been lost.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary action: begin reading the chronicle.
- Secondary action: explore the evidence timeline and source cabinet.
- The remembered line: “History survives in layers.”
- Belief ladder: this history can be reconstructed; different sources carry different weight; oral memory is evidence with its own rules; uncertainty makes the account stronger, not weaker.
- Proof on hand: the supplied 25-page deep-research report, its annotated bibliography, archival portals, peer-reviewed scholarship, official records, court judgments, and the supplied mountain illustration.

## Brand Personality

Grave, lucid, and enduring. The voice is calm enough for difficult evidence, proud without myth-making, and direct enough for younger readers. The emotional goal is recognition: the sense that scattered fragments form a history people can enter and continue.

## Anti-references

No generic AI landing-page grammar, fake parchment, fantasy medieval decoration, glass cards, neon tech styling, patriotic propaganda, or overconfident linear ancestry claims. The site must not turn uncertainty into spectacle or reduce Chechen history to warfare alone.

## Design Principles

1. Treat chronology as a mountain path: readers always know where they are in time.
2. Keep evidence beside the claim, not hidden in an endnote maze.
3. Give oral history dignity while distinguishing memory, inference, and contemporaneous documentation.
4. Build for expansion: every chapter, source, translation, and testimony can be extended without redesign.
5. Let difficult events remain human: name loss, displacement, daily life, culture, and survival alongside political history.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable line lengths, color-independent confidence labels, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and complete Norwegian, English, and Russian interface/content coverage.
